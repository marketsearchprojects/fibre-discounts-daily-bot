£38 more for the things you enjoy.

In this illustrative example, Offer A costs £372 over 12 months before its possible £50 reward. If that reward is received, the first-year net cost becomes £322. Offer B costs £360.

That is a potential £38 first-year saving—10.6% compared with Offer B, with a net monthly equivalent of £26.83 rather than £30. Small choices can add up: put your own quotes into the tool and discover your own result.

These are illustrative prices, not current provider offers. The 12-month view is a budgeting comparison, not a claim that a package has a 12-month minimum term. If your contract lasts longer, compare the remaining commitment too.

Match the connection to the job.

A small saving is not good value if the connection does not fit your household. Consider upload speed as well as download speed, the quoted service at your address, and any equipment or remote-access requirement you actually have. Diagnose a Wi-Fi coverage problem before paying for a faster internet package to solve the wrong issue.
